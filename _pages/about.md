I am a student at the [Yandex School of Data Analysis](https://shad.yandex.ru/) and a graduate of the [Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology (MIPT)](https://mipt.ru/). I work as a junior researcher at the [P. N. Lebedev Physical Institute RAS (LPI RAS)](https://lebedev.ru/) and as a laboratory assistant at the [Pushchino Radio Astronomy Observatory](https://www.prao.ru/). My research sits at the intersection of machine learning and astrophysics: I use domain adaptation and deep learning to bridge simulated and real observational data for morphological classification of parsec-scale AGN jets in VLBI images, advised by Prof. Mikhail Lisakov.

Before turning to machine learning, I trained as a theoretical physicist and astrophysicist (BSc in Applied Mathematics and Physics, MIPT, joint with LPI RAS). That background still shapes my research: I've worked on analytical and numerical models of stellar-wind bubbles and shock structures (published in *PASP*), accretion of interstellar gas onto isolated stellar-mass black holes at the Special Astrophysical Observatory RAS, and the magnetospheres of orthogonal interpulse pulsars at MIPT.
