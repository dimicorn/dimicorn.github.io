I am an MSc student in Computer Science and Computer Engineering (Scientific Programming) at the [Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology (MIPT)](https://mipt.ru/), working at the intersection of machine learning and astrophysics. My research centers on using domain adaptation and deep learning to bridge simulated and real observational data for morphological classification of parsec-scale AGN jets in VLBI images, advised by Prof. Mikhail Lisakov at the P. N. Lebedev Physical Institute RAS.

Before turning to machine learning, I trained as a theoretical physicist and astrophysicist (BSc in Applied Mathematics and Physics, MIPT, joint with the Lebedev Physical Institute). That background still shapes my research: I've worked on analytical and numerical models of stellar-wind bubbles and shock structures (published in *PASP*), accretion of interstellar gas onto isolated stellar-mass black holes at the Special Astrophysical Observatory RAS, and the magnetospheres of orthogonal interpulse pulsars at MIPT.
